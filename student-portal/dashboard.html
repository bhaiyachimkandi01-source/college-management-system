Frequently Asked Questions

How do I view my attendance?

Go to the Attendance section from the sidebar to see subject-wise attendance with percentage bars.

How are SGPA and CGPA calculated?

SGPA is calculated based on your grades per semester. CGPA is the average of all semester SGPAs.

How can I pay my fees?

Visit the Fees section to see your pending dues. Contact the admin office for payment options.

How do I update my profile?

Navigate to Profile in the sidebar, edit your details, and click Save Changes.
